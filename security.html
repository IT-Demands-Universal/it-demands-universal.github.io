Questions people ask

Am I too small to be a target?

No. Attackers mostly go where a door is unlocked. Verizon’s 2026 report points to stolen logins and unpatched internet-facing devices as the common way in, not company size.

Do I need a VPN?

Usually not for everyday browsing at home. A VPN makes sense when staff reach office systems remotely or you often use public Wi-Fi. I’ll tell you honestly whether it’s worth it for you.

How much does it cost?

It’s priced by scope. A home router and password cleanup is a small job; an office with email, backups and a firewall is bigger. You get the price in writing first.

Will this slow my team down?

Barely. Two-step sign-in adds a few seconds. I pick the least annoying method, such as app prompts or passkeys, and test it with you before rolling it out.

What if I think I’ve already been hacked?

Change passwords from a device you trust, call your bank if money is involved, and report it at ic3.gov. I can help secure and rebuild afterward. If customer data may be exposed, Florida sets notice deadlines, so bring in your attorney and insurer early.
